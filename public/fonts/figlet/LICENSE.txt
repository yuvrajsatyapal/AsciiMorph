The Morph Grid glyph designs and the morph-grid.flf font file were authored
specifically for AsciiMorph. Morph Wide doubles the Grid strokes and Morph Slim
uses terminal-style vertical strokes; these original derivatives share this license. No third-party FIGlet glyph artwork was copied.
The lowercase alphabet intentionally shares the uppercase glyph designs.
